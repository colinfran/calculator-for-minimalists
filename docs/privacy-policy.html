Privacy Policy for Calculator for Minimalists

Last updated: March 2026

Calculator for Minimalists is developed and provided by Colin Franceschini as a free application.

Your privacy is important. This app is designed to function entirely on your device and does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information Collection

Calculator for Minimalists does not collect any personal data or user information.

The app does not collect or transmit:

  • Personal information
  • Usage data
  • Device information
  • Analytics data
  • Crash reports
  • Log data
  • Location data

All calculations are performed locally on your device, and no data is sent to any servers.

Third Party Services

Calculator for Minimalists does not use third party analytics, advertising, tracking services, or external SDKs that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Calculator for Minimalists does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
